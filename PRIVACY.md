# Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 4, 2026

CalculatorHQ is designed to be a simple calculator app that respects your privacy.

## Data Collection

CalculatorHQ does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

The app does not require an account, does not ask for contact information, and does not collect identifiers, location data, usage analytics, diagnostics, financial information, contacts, photos, files, or any other personal data.

## Tracking

CalculatorHQ does not track users across apps or websites owned by other companies.

The app does not use third-party advertising networks, tracking SDKs, or cross-app tracking technologies.

## Internet Access

CalculatorHQ does not require an internet connection for its calculator features.

## Local App Data

Some calculator information, such as calculation history or app preferences, may be stored locally on your device to support app functionality. This information remains on your device and is not sent to the developer or any third party by CalculatorHQ.

You can remove locally stored app data by clearing the app's data where supported by your device, or by deleting the app.

## Children's Privacy

CalculatorHQ does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

## Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if CalculatorHQ's functionality changes. Any updates will be posted in this file.
